About Our Academy

Forging Officers of Tomorrow

Excel Defence Academy prepares ambitious students for careers in the armed forces. We combine structured academics, defence examination coaching, physical conditioning and personality development into a single, disciplined environment.

From the parade ground to the study hall, every part of the day is designed to build character, stamina and the confidence to lead.

  • NDA preparation
  • CDS preparation
  • AFCAT preparation
  • Agniveer preparation
  • Jalveer preparation
  • Vavuveer preparation
  • MPC with IPE examination
  • SSB interview preparation
  • Physical fitness training
  • Personality development

Our mission is to develop disciplined, confident and responsible future officers who are ready to serve the nation with pride.
